Live pipeline/ws/mic → /ws

20 ms frames→detector→splicer→listener
  • Detector: rule-based on the CPU (DTW repeat match, steady-sound prolongations), or Best (GPU): teacher windows sent to the worker, a rule-based stand-in running alongside
  • Splicer: delay 0.25–1.14 s; never plays audio not yet judged; skips cut spans with 8 ms fades; the room's own tone, never digital silence; lag rebuilt in natural pauses
  • Sensitivity scales every detector's thresholds; cut limits grow with it
  • Captions: NVIDIA FastConformer streaming (sherpa-onnx, int8) on the CPU

Recording enginesNatural · Thorough · Live

teacher probabilitiesverbatim words→word-aligned cuts→Nemotron guard
  • Cuts land only between words (CrisperWhisper timings): no real word is sliced
  • Nemotron reviews every proposed cut and keeps the repeats that were meant
  • Thorough also cuts um, uh and repeated phrases, and shrinks long pauses to under 0.5 s
  • Rendered with crossfades and room tone; Live on a recording: the live model's cuts with the time closed up

GPU 0 · live teacher (v1)

  • WavLM-Large + BiGRU head, 5 classes per 20 ms frame
  • Sliding windows: 4 s of context + 200 ms lookahead (Gentle) or 500 ms (Strong); a decision every 160 ms
  • Round trip from Mumbai: p90 ≈ 0.3 s, so Best (GPU) runs 0.84 s behind you

GPU 1 · recordings

  • Teacher v2 on 30 s chunks, fp16
  • CrisperWhisper 2.0 large: verbatim words with timings, stutters included; encoder run without attention maps (4.0 GB peak, not 6.8)
  • 18.5 s of audio analysed in 4.7 s
Only GPU work runs here. Cut logic, the meaning guard, captions and rendering stay on the app server. Weights are mounted read-only from private Kaggle notebooks and never published; nothing is kept on the machine.

Sensitivity
0: clear stutters only50: as measured100: cuts aggressively
GPU model, Gentle: removes 12% → 33% → 60% of stutters at 0 / 50 / 100 (0.6% → 3.1% → 10% of words lost); Strong reaches 77%. Above 50 a cut may run longer before it counts as a detector error.

Models & training

  • Teacher (v1 live, v2 recordings): WavLM-Large + BiGRU head → per 20 ms frame: sound repetition, word repetition, prolongation, interjection, and block (reported, never cut)
  • Trained on Kaggle GPUs: SEP-28k, FluencyBank, LibriSpeech with synthetic stutters spliced in, and silver labels from CrisperWhisper verbatim transcripts of real stuttered speech
  • Causal student (CNN + GRU, ONNX int8) for laptops without a GPU; rule-based detector as the fallback that always runs
  • SEP-28k-E (speaker-exclusive): macro F1 0.618; AUC 0.98 for word repetitions, 0.96 for fillers
